Official Sponsor

IEEE UP Section supports ICACSDF 2026 to strengthen research, collaboration, and innovation across cybersecurity and digital forensics.

Why Sponsor ICACSDF 2026?

Sponsorship offers meaningful engagement with researchers, students, and industry professionals.

High Visibility
Brand exposure on the conference website, banners, stage screens, and digital promotions.
Direct Networking
Connect with authors, keynote speakers, and participants working on real security problems.
Hiring & Talent
Engage with students and researchers for internships, placements, and collaborations.
Speaking Opportunities
Sponsor talks, panel sessions, workshops, and product showcases (as per package).
Thought Leadership
Position your organization at the forefront of cyber defense and digital investigation.
Partnerships
Create joint research, product collaborations, MoUs, and industry-academia initiatives.

Sponsorship Tiers

Use these tiers as a clean structure. Replace benefits or add pricing once finalized.

Title Sponsor
Highest Visibility
  • Logo on all major branding (stage, website, backdrops)
  • Keynote/featured session slot (as approved)
  • Premium exhibition booth
  • Top placement in all promotions
Platinum Sponsor
Strong Branding
  • Logo on website & banners
  • Session sponsorship / panel branding
  • Exhibition booth
  • Social media promotions
Gold Sponsor
Visibility
  • Logo placement on website & venue
  • Brand mention during sessions
  • Booth / desk (as per availability)
  • Promotional inserts in kit
Silver Sponsor
Support
  • Logo on website sponsor section
  • Brand mention in acknowledgements
  • Option to provide standees (approved)
Academic Partner
Academia
  • Institution logo on website
  • Student participation support (as applicable)
  • Collaboration opportunities with UPES
Startup / Community Partner
Community
  • Logo + partner mention
  • Community booth (subject to space)
  • Social media shoutouts
