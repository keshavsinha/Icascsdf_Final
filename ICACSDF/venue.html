How to Reach

Dehradun is well-connected by air, rail, and road. Use this guide to plan your travel.

By Air

Nearest airport: Dehradun Airport (Jolly Grant). From the airport, you can take a taxi/cab to UPES Bidholi Campus.

By Rail

Major railhead: Dehradun Railway Station. Local cabs and app-based taxis are available for campus commute.

By Road

Dehradun has strong road connectivity from Delhi/NCR and nearby cities. You can reach via bus, taxi, or self-drive.
