Call for Papers

We invite high-quality, original, and unpublished research contributions in the areas of Cyber Security and Digital Forensics. Submissions may include conceptual, constructive, empirical, experimental, or theoretical research that advances the state-of-the-art in these domains. The conference will be conducted as an in-person event.

Authors are expected to clearly articulate the technical and scientific contributions of their work, along with a well-defined problem context, including system models, environments, threat assumptions, datasets, and evaluation methodologies. All submissions must be written in English and adhere to standard academic and formatting guidelines.

ICACSDF 2026 aims to bring together leading researchers, practitioners, and industry experts to exchange ideas and discuss emerging challenges in cybersecurity, digital forensics, and information assurance. The conference will provide a platform for presenting innovative solutions, exploring defensive engineering strategies, advancing investigation techniques, and showcasing real-world security deployments.

Submission Guidelines

Clear requirements to help authors submit a strong, review-ready manuscript.

Manuscript Requirements

  • Original, unpublished, and not under review elsewhere
  • Written in English with clear structure (abstract → methods → results)
  • Clearly state contribution, evaluation metrics, and limitations
  • Figures/tables must be readable and properly referenced
  • At least one author must register and present if accepted

Ethics & Similarity Policy

  • Plagiarism and duplicate submission are strictly prohibited
  • All sources must be cited; use quotes where applicable
  • Disclose conflicts of interest and author contributions
  • Submissions may be screened via similarity-check tools

How to Submit

Submit your manuscript through the official submission portal (e.g., Microsoft CMT) once available on the conference website. Ensure that your PDF is review-ready, anonymization rules (if any) are followed, and all co-authors have approved the submission.
