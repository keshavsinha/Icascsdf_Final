We invite proposals on cutting-edge and interdisciplinary topics, including but not limited to:

Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning Data Science & Big Data Analytics Robotics & Intelligent Automation IoT & Cyber-Physical Systems Embedded Systems & VLSI Design Cloud Computing & Distributed Systems Cybersecurity, Blockchain & Trustworthy Systems Smart Cities & Industry 4.0 Healthcare Technologies & Medical IoT Green Computing & Sustainable Technologies Emerging Interdisciplinary Research Areas

This list is indicative. Proposals on other relevant and innovative topics in computing, security, and digital forensics are equally welcome.

📌 Guidelines for Special Session Proposals

To ensure quality, diversity, and successful execution, the following guidelines must be strictly followed.

Submission Requirements

  • All papers undergo rigorous peer-review
  • Minimum of 10 accepted and registered papers required
  • Organizer co-authored papers must not exceed 60% of total submissions
  • Each author may submit a maximum of 2 papers
  • No more than 30% of papers from the organizers' own institution
  • Insufficient sessions may be merged with regular tracks

Roles & Responsibilities

As a Special Session Organizer, you will:

  • Define a focused and impactful session theme
  • Promote your session through academic and professional networks
  • Attract high-quality research contributions
  • Coordinate with the Technical Program Committee during review
  • Ensure timely communication and smooth session execution

📩 How to Apply

Follow these simple steps to submit your Special Session Proposal.

1
Download the Proposal Form

Download the official Special Session Proposal Form (PDF) and fill it out completely before submitting.

2
Prepare Your Proposal

Include Session Title, Brief Description & Objectives, Scope and Topics Covered, and complete Organizer Details (Name, Affiliation, Contact).

3
Get HOD Signature

Obtain the signature of your Head of Department on the proposal form as required by the conference guidelines.

4
Submit via Email

Email the completed proposal form to:

ayush.agrawal@ddn.upes.ac.in

Proposal Must Include

  • Session Title
  • Brief Description and Objectives
  • Scope and Topics Covered
  • Organizer 1 & 2 Details (Name, Affiliation, Email, Contact)
  • Brief Biography of Organizer(s)
  • Technical Description of the Session
  • Motivation and Relevance
  • Potential Authors and Reviewers (Optional)
  • Expected Number of Submissions
  • Signature of HOD

All accepted papers in Special Sessions will be included in the official conference proceedings, subject to registration and presentation guidelines.
